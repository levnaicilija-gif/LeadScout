What it never does

Most "AI lead tools" invent contacts and call them verified. LeadScout is built on the opposite rule.

Never fabricates
  • No invented names, emails, phone numbers or job openings — every field has a source page, a pattern label, or is empty.
  • "Verified" is set only by a fetcher that read it from the named source.
  • Every source is re-checked each morning and can be confirmed by you.
Never acts alone
  • Nothing is sent to a client or candidate without a recruiter pressing send.
  • A CV with a name on it cannot leave the system. An unverified certificate cannot go in a pack.
  • Revealing a full profile is a logged action — who, when, which client.
