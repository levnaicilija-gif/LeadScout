19. COMPOUND SIGNAL SCORING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A company hit by multiple independent signals in a short window (a tender
award, a news mention, a Hiring now posting, a repost) is a stronger hiring-wave
indicator than any single signal alone. Right now each lead is scored
independently with no awareness of a company's other concurrent signals.

Definition of "independent signal" for a company, within a rolling 60-day
window:
1. A tender-award lead (from TED)
2. A news-sourced won-work lead
3. An open Hiring now posting
4. A posting re-advertised (already tracked by item 17's priority-raise logic)

Scoring change:
- When a company has 2+ of these signal types active within the window, boost
  the fit/pressure score of ALL of that company's leads/postings — don't just
  boost one and leave the others unchanged.
- The boost amount and its reasoning must be visible and explained on the lead/
  posting itself (e.g. "boosted: also has an active Hiring now posting" or
  "boosted: tender award + news mention, 12 days apart") — same transparency
  standard as "why this score" elsewhere. Never a silent number change.
- This does NOT invent a new score from nothing — it's a multiplier/boost on
  top of the existing fit/pressure calculation, using only signals already
  captured in the database. No model call needed — pure logic over data
  already stored.
- A company with only one signal type is entirely unaffected — no penalty for
  having just one signal, only a boost for having more than one.

Report: how many current companies have 2+ concurrent signal types, and what
their boosted vs. unboosted scores look like — a real before/after sample, not
just a count.

Commit after each step, two lines per step. Full release gate before every
commit, screen checks as a signed-in user, both widths. Report the sample
before/after and confirm the boost reasoning is visible on screen, not hidden
in the calculation.

Decision on item 19 (owner, 2026-09-15): signal types 3 and 4 are ONE type. A
re-advertised posting is a stronger version of "has an open posting", not
independent corroboration from a separate source. A company reaches the 2+
threshold only by combining a posting with something genuinely separate — a
tender award or a news mention — never by reposting the same job. Three types
in all: tender award, news mention, open posting (re-adverts included).
