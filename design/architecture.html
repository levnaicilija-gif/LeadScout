1. Signup & onboarding

A new customer's first choice — their industry — is also their entitlement boundary. It decides what they see from the shared pool from the first second, and which trade/buyer configuration applies to them.

flowchart TD
    A[New customer signs up] --> B[Choose ONE industry]
    B --> C[Workspace created — industry entitlement set]
    C --> D[Day 1: trade cards + glossary unlock]
    D --> E{Onboarding day advances?}
    E -->|Not built yet| F[Stays on Day 1]
    E -->|Planned| G[Day N: full tool unlocks]
    C --> H[Sees shared-pool data for that industry immediately]

onboarding_day currently never advances anywhere in the app — a real gap, more important now that a genuine "zero to hero" path is part of the product's mission, not just an RFBT convenience.

2. The shared discovery pool

Discovery is crawled once and shared. Every customer sees the same underlying facts, filtered to their own industry. Candidates, CVs, notes and outreach are never shared — those stay private per workspace.

flowchart TD
    S1[News sources — ~610 sites] --> R[Radar: read + qualify each article]
    S2[TED tender awards] --> T[Extract: CPV match OR buyer-allowlist match]
    S3[Job boards + company career pages] --> J[Job-post crawl]
    R --> P[(Shared pool: leads, companies, contacts)]
    T --> P
    J --> P
    P --> F{Filtered by the customer's chosen industry}
    F --> W1[Workspace: RFBT]
    F --> W2[Workspace: Customer B]
    F --> W3[Workspace: Customer C]

Design agreed, not yet built. Candidates, verifications, outreach and notes stay strictly private per workspace — an enforced boundary, not incidental.

3. Won-work leads — extraction & scoring

A lead only exists if a real, contactable person at the winning company is named. The buyer-allowlist rule below — keeping an award when a recognised industry buyer (Ørsted, Energinet…) sits behind a contract filed under a generic code — is built and measured but not yet live; see the note under the diagram.

flowchart TD
    A[Article or TED award] --> B{Company won something AND a named, contactable person quoted?}
    B -->|No| X[Rejected — reason recorded]
    B -->|Yes, and CPV matches a trade| C[Kept]
    B -->|Yes, and buyer is on the allowlist + CPV is construction/repair| C
    C --> D[Fit score]
    D --> E{2+ signal types? award + news + open posting}
    E -->|Yes| F[Boosted fit]
    E -->|No| G[Base fit]
    F --> H[Shown in Leads / Today]
    G --> H

The buyer-allowlist branch is built and measured, not yet live. On the open TED corpus it is worth +74% (35 → 61 trade-relevant awards per 60 days across six countries), and all 20 additions were read individually. Production cannot reach that yet: the ingest's own query pre-filters TED on classification-cpv IN (TRADE_CPV), so an award carrying none of the 42 trade codes anywhere is never fetched and no gate can keep it — measured, production would see 3 of 23. A second, targeted buyer-name query for the allowlisted buyers is required before the rule reaches its measured effect.

4. Hiring Now

Open job postings, grouped by company, with a real contact found automatically wherever possible.

flowchart TD
    A[Job board or company careers page] --> B[Crawl the posting]
    B --> C[Group postings by company]
    C --> D[Find a contact: careers page, leadership page, search]
    D --> E[Pressure score: how many openings, how fresh]
    E --> F[Shown in Hiring Now / Today]

5. Certificate Check & Verify / CV matching

Certificate Check is deliberately narrow — nothing there ever becomes a candidate. Verify is where a real candidate record is built, from a CV, a certificate, or both together.

flowchart TD
    A[A document is dropped] --> B{Where, and what?}
    B -->|Certificate only, on Certificate Check| C[Decode + check issuer register]
    C --> D[Stored, unattached — no candidate created]
    B -->|CV, on Verify| E[Candidate created]
    B -->|CV + certificate together, on Verify| E
    B -->|Certificate only, on Verify| F{Matches an existing candidate by name?}
    F -->|Yes| G[Attached to that candidate — jobs re-matched]
    F -->|No| H[New 'unconfirmed' candidate from name + trade]
    E --> I[Top 3-5 matching jobs auto-suggested]
    G --> I
    H --> I
    I --> J[Manual 'match one specific job' still available]

Auto-suggested matching is scoped and investigated, not yet built. A certificate dropped alone on Certificate Check never creates a candidate — by design.

6. The Today page

Two different views of the same underlying data — one curated and capped, one complete and uncapped.

flowchart TD
    A[Today page] --> B{Toggle}
    B -->|Priority — default| C[Top 6 leads + top 5 hiring, ranked by fit, capped]
    B -->|Last 24 hours| D[Everything from the last 24h — uncapped]
    D --> E[Grouped by source, hottest first within each]
    C --> F[Click an item → opens Leads, filtered to just that]
    E --> F
